# CyberShield — Annual Security Awareness Calendar

## January: New Year, New Security Habits

**Theme:** Fresh Start — Reset and Reinforce  
**Featured Training Module:** Password Security & MFA Fundamentals  
**Phishing Simulation:** Fake New Year HR benefits enrollment email  
**Campaign Activity:** "Password Audit Week" — Encourage all employees to update passwords and enable MFA on all work accounts  
**Newsletter Topic:** "5 Security Resolutions That Actually Work"  
**Poster/Infographic:** "Your New Year's Security Checklist" — MFA, strong password, clean desk, screen lock  
**Gamification Challenge:** First employee in each department to complete the MFA setup earns a badge  
**Manager Talking Points:** "Let's start the year by making sure everyone's access is secure. Please remind your team to update their Microsoft Authenticator this week."

---

## February: Love Your Data

**Theme:** Data Privacy — Protect What Matters  
**Featured Training Module:** Data Privacy & Confidentiality / PII Handling  
**Phishing Simulation:** Valentine's Day e-card with malicious attachment ("Someone sent you a surprise!")  
**Campaign Activity:** "Data Privacy Week" — coincides with International Privacy Day (Jan 28); show employees what data the company holds and why it is protected  
**Newsletter Topic:** "Why Client Data Is Our Most Precious Asset"  
**Poster/Infographic:** "The Anatomy of PII — What Personal Data Looks Like and Why It Must Be Protected"  
**Gamification Challenge:** Highest scorer on the Data Privacy quiz earns "Data Guardian" badge  
**Manager Talking Points:** "Our clients trust us with their data. Let's talk about what that means in practical terms for our team."

---

## March: Clean Up Your Digital Life

**Theme:** Spring Cleaning for Your Security Posture  
**Featured Training Module:** Clean Desk Policy / Physical Security  
**Phishing Simulation:** Fake IT spring clean notification — "click here to verify your active accounts"  
**Campaign Activity:** "Digital Spring Clean" — clean out old shared drives, revoke unnecessary access, archive old projects  
**Newsletter Topic:** "The Hidden Risks in Your Shared Drive: A Spring Clean Guide"  
**Poster/Infographic:** "Your Clean Desk Checklist" — lock screen, documents away, headset off, screen privacy filter  
**Gamification Challenge:** Department that completes the most course modules in March wins a team lunch  
**Manager Talking Points:** "Let's take this month to review who has access to what in our team's shared folders. I'll book 30 minutes for a team access review."

## April: No Fooling Around with Phishing

**Theme:** Phishing Awareness Month  
**Featured Training Module:** Phishing & Social Engineering Defense  
**Phishing Simulation:** April Fools' themed — "You've won a prize" email / CEO gift card request  
**Campaign Activity:** "Phish Tank" — display department phishing leaderboard showing report rates; celebrate the top reporters  
**Newsletter Topic:** "Real Phishing Emails We Almost Fell For: An Honest Debrief"  
**Poster/Infographic:** "Anatomy of a Phishing Email" — labelled real phishing screenshot with red flags highlighted  
**Gamification Challenge:** "Phish Finder" badge awarded to employees who report a simulated phishing email within 10 minutes  
**Manager Talking Points:** "Phishing is the #1 way attackers get in. This month we're sharpening our team's eye for suspicious emails. Encourage your team to report anything that feels off."

## May: World Password Day — Password Power Month

**Theme:** Authentication Security (World Password Day = first Thursday in May)  
**Featured Training Module:** Password Security & MFA / Conditional Access Awareness  
**Phishing Simulation:** Fake "Your password will expire in 2 days" credential harvesting email  
**Campaign Activity:** World Password Day challenge — audit your password manager, update any reused passwords, verify all work accounts have MFA  
**Newsletter Topic:** "Passphrases vs. Passwords: Why Length Beats Complexity"  
**Poster/Infographic:** "Password Strength Visual Guide" — examples of weak vs. strong vs. passphrase passwords  
**Gamification Challenge:** First 50 employees to complete the MFA module earn double XP  
**Manager Talking Points:** "This Thursday is World Password Day. Let's make sure everyone on the team is using the company password manager and has MFA on all work accounts."

## October: Cybersecurity Awareness Month (FULL CAMPAIGN)

**Theme:** "Secure Our World" (aligned with CISA/NCSA theme)  
**Featured Training Modules:** Full release of AI & Modern Threats track; BPO Security track reinforcement  
**Phishing Simulations:** Two simulations this month — one email, one QR code or SMS  

**Week 1 — Password & Identity Security Week:**
- Campaign: MFA challenge — get 100% MFA coverage this week
- Event: Virtual webinar "AI Threats and What They Mean for You"
- Prize: Gift cards for top weekly quiz scorer

**Week 2 — Phishing Awareness Week:**
- Campaign: Phish leaderboard competition — which team reports the most simulations?
- Event: "Spot the Phish" lunch-and-learn session
- Poster: Real anonymized phishing examples from the past year

**Week 3 — Data Privacy & BPO Security Week:**
- Campaign: "PII Treasure Hunt" — employees identify PII risks in mock scenarios
- Event: Manager-led team discussion: "How do we handle client data?"
- Newsletter: Client data handling dos and don'ts

**Week 4 — AI & Future Threats Week:**
- Campaign: AI deepfake awareness video shared company-wide
- Event: "AI and Your Job" discussion — benefits vs. risks
- Gamification: Halloween-themed "Trick or Phish" quiz challenge

**Newsletter Topic:** Full October edition — "Your October Security Roundup: Wins, Lessons, and What's Next"  
**Manager Talking Points:** "October is our biggest security month. Please champion this with your team — their participation directly improves our company's security posture and our client compliance scores."
