# CyberShield — Employee Communication Templates

All templates use the CyberShield brand tone: professional, friendly, encouraging, and human. Avoid jargon. Keep it short.

---

## 1. Welcome to CyberShield (Onboarding Email)

**Subject:** Welcome to CyberShield — Your Security Training Starts Here 🛡️  
**From:** security-noreply@company.com  
**To:** {employee_email}

---

Hi {first_name},

Welcome to the team! We're excited to have you, and your first task is a fun one.

**Your CyberShield security training is ready.**

CyberShield is our internal security awareness platform — it's where you'll build the skills to keep yourself, your colleagues, and our clients safe online.

**Here's how to get started:**
1. Go to: https://cybershield.company.com
2. Click "Sign in with Microsoft" — use your company account
3. Complete your first course: **Core Cybersecurity Awareness** (about 45 minutes)

Your onboarding training must be completed within **14 days**. It's engaging, practical, and comes with a certificate at the end!

**Questions?** Reply to this email or contact IT Support at helpdesk@company.com.

See you on the leaderboard,  
The CyberShield Team

## 8. Phishing Simulation — Educational Follow-Up (Clicked)

**From:** security-noreply@company.com

---

Hi {first_name},

This is an important but friendly message from the IT Security team.

**You recently clicked a simulated phishing link** that was part of our regular security awareness program. Don't worry — no harm was done. This was a controlled test, and you're not in trouble.

**What happened:**
The email you received was a simulated phishing test. It was designed to look like {scenario_description}. Real phishing emails use the same techniques to steal credentials or deliver malware.

**What to watch for next time:**
{red_flags_list}

**What you should do when you see a real suspicious email:**
1. Do not click any links or open attachments
2. Click the **"Report Phishing"** button in Outlook
3. Delete the email

**Take 5 minutes to complete this short training:**  
[PHISHING AWARENESS MICROLEARNING →](https://cybershield.company.com/courses/phishing-defense)

You're not alone — phishing attacks fool everyone. The important thing is learning from it. Thank you for participating in our security program.

The IT Security Team
