# Safe AI Usage Policy

**Document Number:** SEC-POL-008  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2024  
**Review Date:** April 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Information Security Team + IT Department

---

## 1. Purpose

This Policy establishes guidelines for the safe, ethical, and compliant use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in the workplace. As AI tools become increasingly capable and accessible, this Policy ensures employees can leverage their productivity benefits while protecting client data, Company confidential information, and compliance obligations.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users who use AI tools — including generative AI, language models, AI assistants, and AI-powered features within existing software — in connection with Company business.

---

## 3. Approved AI Tools

The following tools are approved for use. Only tools on this list may be used for Company business:

| Tool | Approved Use Cases | Data Classification Permitted |
|---|---|---|
| **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (enterprise) | Drafting emails, summarizing meetings, code assistance, data analysis within M365 | Up to Confidential |
| **GitHub Copilot** (enterprise) | Code completion and generation for IT/Development staff only | Internal only |
| **[Company-specific approved tool]** | As specified in IT tooling register | As specified |

**All other AI tools are unapproved for business use**, including but not limited to:
- Personal/free tier ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- Personal Google Gemini
- Personal Claude (Anthropic) accounts
- Character.ai, Perplexity (personal tier), Midjourney, DALL-E (non-enterprise)
- Browser-based AI extensions (Grammarly AI, etc.) — unless individually approved by IT

Employees who want to use a specific AI tool not on this list must submit a Tool Approval Request to IT Security.

---

## 4. Data Classification and AI — Non-Negotiable Rules

The following rules apply to ALL AI tools, including approved tools:

### NEVER input into any AI tool:
- **Client PII:** Names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, account numbers, identification numbers of clients' customers
- **Payment card data:** Card numbers, CVV codes, expiry dates
- **Healthcare data (PHI):** Patient names, diagnoses, medication information
- **Confidential business information:** Unreleased financial results, M&A activity, proprietary algorithms
- **Authentication credentials:** Passwords, API keys, tokens, certificates
- **Restricted internal documents:** Board minutes, legal correspondence, HR records

### Permitted in Microsoft 365 Copilot (Confidential and below):
- Internal reports and presentations (non-client PII)
- Meeting transcripts and summaries
- Internal project communications
- Code for internal systems (no hardcoded secrets)

### Permitted in any approved AI tool (Internal and below):
- General business writing and editing
- Research on publicly available topics
- Process documentation using generic examples
- Training content creation (without real client examples)

---

## 5. Prompt Injection Awareness

AI tools that process external content (documents, emails, web pages) may be vulnerable to **prompt injection attacks**: hidden instructions embedded in external content that cause the AI to take unintended actions.

**Employee responsibilities:**
- Be cautious when using AI to summarize, analyze, or process documents received from external parties
- Review AI outputs critically — especially when the AI was processing external content
- Report unexpected or suspicious AI behavior to IT Security
- Never grant AI tools permissions to send emails, access files, or take actions on your behalf without understanding the full implications

---

## 6. Intellectual Property and Copyright

- Content generated by AI tools may be subject to copyright restrictions depending on training data
- Do not use AI to reproduce copyrighted text, images, or code from third parties
- Disclose AI assistance in documents where required (e.g., legal filings, academic submissions, client deliverables where AI disclosure is contractually required)
- AI-generated content must be reviewed and validated by a human before submission as authoritative business output

---

## 7. AI-Generated Content — Quality and Accuracy

AI language models can generate confident-sounding but factually incorrect information ("hallucination").

**Mandatory review requirements:**
- **Never** submit AI-generated compliance reports, legal documents, or client-facing content without thorough human review
- **Never** rely on AI for regulatory, medical, legal, or financial advice without expert verification
- **Always** check AI-cited sources independently — AI models can fabricate citations
- Apply common sense: if an AI response seems too convenient or surprising, verify it

---

## 8. Shadow AI (Unauthorized AI Use)

Using unapproved AI tools for Company business — even with good intentions — constitutes Shadow IT and is a violation of this Policy and the Acceptable Use Policy.

**Shadow AI risks:**
- Vendor's AI model may be trained on data you submit (personal tiers)
- Data exits the Company's security perimeter and contractual protections
- Client contracts may explicitly prohibit third-party AI processing of their data
- GDPR/PDPA obligations for personal data may be violated

**Self-reporting:** Employees who have been using unapproved AI tools should report to IT Security. Self-reporting is treated as a learning opportunity — not a disciplinary matter on first occurrence.

---

## 9. Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:
- Ensuring their team is aware of and trained on this Policy
- Approving exceptions via the IT Exception Request process
- Monitoring for shadow AI usage within their team
- Reporting AI-related security concerns to IT Security

---

## 10. Exception Process

To request use of an unapproved AI tool:
1. Submit a Tool Approval Request via the IT Service Desk portal
2. Provide: tool name, vendor, use case, data classification of data to be used
3. IT Security reviews privacy terms, security controls, and compliance implications within 5 business days
4. Approved tools are added to the official list with defined conditions of use

---

## 11. Policy Violations

Violations of this Policy are treated under the Acceptable Use Policy. Introducing client PII into an unauthorized AI tool may additionally constitute a data breach with regulatory and contractual consequences.

---

## 12. Review

This Policy will be reviewed semi-annually given the rapid evolution of AI technology and regulations. Employees will be notified of significant updates and required to re-acknowledge.
