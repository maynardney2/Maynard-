# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document Number:** SEC-POL-001  
**Version:** 2.3  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Review Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Information Security Team  
**Approver:** Chief Information Security Officer

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") establishes the rules and guidelines for the appropriate use of the Company's information technology systems, networks, internet access, email services, devices, and data. This Policy exists to protect employees, clients, and the Company from security breaches, legal liability, and reputational harm that may result from misuse of Company systems.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:
- All full-time and part-time employees
- Contractors, consultants, and temporary workers
- Third-party vendors with access to Company systems
- All company-owned and company-managed devices
- Personal devices used to access Company resources (BYOD)

This Policy applies regardless of location — whether working in a Company office, at home, or any other remote location.

---

## 3. Acceptable Use of Company Systems

Employees are authorized to use Company technology resources for legitimate business purposes. Acceptable uses include:

- Performing assigned job duties and responsibilities
- Communicating with colleagues, clients, and vendors through approved channels
- Accessing and processing data required to complete work assignments
- Participating in Company-approved training and professional development
- Using approved software applications for business tasks

Incidental personal use is permitted where it does not interfere with job performance, does not consume excessive resources, and complies with all other provisions of this Policy.

---

## 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Company systems and networks:

### 4.1 Security Violations
- Attempting to gain unauthorized access to any system, account, or network
- Installing unauthorized software, including free browser extensions or AI tools not approved by IT
- Disabling, modifying, or circumventing security controls including antivirus, firewall, or MDM enrollment
- Sharing login credentials, passwords, or access tokens with any other person
- Using another employee's credentials to access any system, even with their permission
- Attempting to intercept or monitor network traffic

### 4.2 Data Handling Violations
- Accessing, copying, or transmitting data that is not required for your job role
- Sending confidential or restricted data to personal email accounts (Gmail, Yahoo, etc.)
- Uploading Company or client data to unauthorized cloud services (personal Dropbox, Google Drive, etc.)
- Taking screenshots or recordings of client data on non-company devices
- Printing confidential data and leaving it unattended or disposing of it without shredding

### 4.3 Inappropriate Content
- Accessing, downloading, or distributing pornographic, obscene, or offensive material
- Creating, sending, or forwarding content that constitutes harassment, discrimination, or bullying
- Accessing gambling websites or online gaming platforms for personal use during work hours
- Streaming personal entertainment (movies, music, gaming) that impacts network performance

### 4.4 Legal Violations
- Downloading, distributing, or using unlicensed software or media (piracy)
- Violating intellectual property rights or copyright laws
- Engaging in any activity that constitutes fraud, deception, or misrepresentation
- Using Company systems to conduct personal business or commercial activities
- Violating any applicable law, regulation, or contractual obligation

### 4.5 System Integrity
- Installing or running cryptocurrency mining software
- Participating in distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, even if directed by a third party
- Modifying system logs or audit records
- Deliberately introducing malware, viruses, or other malicious code

---

## 5. Internet Use

Employees have access to the internet for business purposes. The Company may use content filtering to block categories of websites that pose security or productivity risks.

**Permitted internet use:**
- Research related to job functions
- Accessing client portals and approved vendor websites
- Professional development and industry news

**Restricted internet use:**
- Social media during work hours (limited to approved business accounts for business purposes)
- Personal shopping or financial transactions on Company devices
- Accessing sites blocked by the Company's content filter

---

## 6. Email Use

Company email accounts are provided for business communication. Email users are responsible for:

- Using professional language and tone in all business communications
- Not forwarding confidential client emails outside the Company without authorization
- Applying appropriate sensitivity labels to emails containing sensitive data
- Reporting suspicious emails using the "Report Phishing" button before deleting
- Not subscribing to personal newsletters or mailing lists using work email addresses

---

## 7. Social Media Policy

Employees must not:
- Represent themselves as speaking on behalf of the Company without explicit authorization
- Share confidential Company or client information on any social media platform
- Post photographs of client data, Company systems, or confidential documents
- Disparage clients, partners, or colleagues on social media in connection with their employment

Employees may share general Company news and their professional achievements with appropriate discretion.

---

## 8. Monitoring Disclosure

**Employees should have no expectation of privacy** when using Company-owned devices, networks, or systems. The Company reserves the right to monitor, intercept, and review all activity on Company technology resources for business, security, and compliance purposes. This includes but is not limited to:

- Email communications sent or received via Company email
- Internet browsing history on Company devices
- Files stored on Company systems, including OneDrive and SharePoint
- Network traffic traversing Company infrastructure
- Application usage logs and system event logs

Monitoring is conducted in compliance with applicable laws and is intended to protect both the Company and its employees.

---

## 9. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Policy may result in:

- Verbal or written warning
- Suspension of technology access
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment
- Civil or criminal legal proceedings where applicable
- Notification to relevant regulatory authorities

The severity of the consequence will be proportionate to the nature and intent of the violation, the potential or actual harm caused, and the employee's history of policy compliance.

---

## 10. Reporting Violations

If you observe or suspect a violation of this Policy, you are required to report it to:
- Your direct manager, or
- The IT Security team at security@company.com, or
- The HR department for HR-related violations

Reports may be made anonymously through the Company's Ethics Hotline. Retaliation against employees who make good-faith reports is strictly prohibited.

---

## 11. Policy Review

This Policy will be reviewed annually or following any significant change in technology, legal requirements, or security incidents. Employees will be notified of material changes via company email and will be required to re-acknowledge the updated Policy.

---

## 12. Acknowledgment

By continuing to access and use Company technology systems, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy. Formal acknowledgment is required via the CyberShield training portal.
