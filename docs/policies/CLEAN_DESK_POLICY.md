# Clean Desk & Screen Lock Policy

**Document Number:** SEC-POL-006  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Information Security Team

---

## 1. Purpose

This Policy ensures that confidential information is not left visible or accessible when a workstation is unattended, protecting client data, employee information, and Company intellectual property from unauthorized access, theft, or inadvertent disclosure.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees working in Company offices, client sites, or shared workspaces. Remote workers must apply equivalent controls at their home workspace.

---

## 3. Clean Desk Requirements

When leaving your workstation — even briefly — the following must be secured:

**Documents & Physical Media:**
- All paper documents containing confidential or restricted data must be placed face-down or locked in a secure drawer
- Client documents must be locked away at the end of every shift — never left on the desk overnight
- USB drives, SD cards, CDs, and any external media must be stored in a locked drawer
- Visitor badges and access cards must not be left visible on the desk when unattended

**Printed Materials:**
- Collect printed documents immediately from printers — do not leave them unattended
- Use secure print (PIN release) when printing confidential documents
- Shred documents before disposal — do not put them in regular waste bins

**End of Day:**
- Desk must be clear of all papers, documents, and media at the end of each workday
- Lock all drawers and cabinets
- Disconnect headsets and personal devices
- Ensure monitor does not face a window or public area where passers-by can view content

---

## 4. Screen Lock Requirements

**Automatic Screen Lock:**
- Company-managed devices are configured by Intune to lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity
- Do not change or disable screen lock settings

**Manual Screen Lock (Required when leaving your desk):**
- Windows: Press **Windows Key + L** immediately when stepping away
- Mac: Press **Control + Command + Q** or set a hot corner
- Even a 2-minute bathroom break: lock your screen

**Strong Authentication for Unlock:**
- Screen unlock must require your Windows Hello PIN, fingerprint, or face recognition
- Do not disable Windows Hello in favor of a simple 4-digit PIN
- Do not write your screen PIN/password on sticky notes near your workstation

**Shared Workstations (Hot Desking):**
- Always log out (not just lock) at the end of your session on a shared workstation
- Clear browser history and saved credentials before logging out
- Do not save passwords in the browser on shared devices

---

## 5. Physical Screen Privacy

**Screen Position:**
- Position your screen so it cannot be viewed by passers-by, visitors, or colleagues unless necessary
- In open-plan offices: use privacy screen filters on monitors displaying client data
- In client BPO environments: all screens displaying client data must have privacy filters fitted

**Camera and Recording:**
- Be aware of webcam position during video calls — ensure no confidential documents are visible behind you
- Do not position your screen near windows where it may be captured in photos or reflections
- When screen sharing in Teams/Zoom: share only the specific window needed, not your full screen

---

## 6. Visitor Management

- Visitors must not be left alone in secured work areas
- Lock your screen and turn documents face-down before escorting a visitor past your workstation
- Do not allow visitors to take photographs in secure areas without explicit management approval
- Report any unauthorized visitors or tailgating incidents to the security team

---

## 7. Remote Work Application

Employees working from home must:
- Position their workstation so household members and visitors cannot view the screen
- Use a privacy screen filter if working in public locations (coffee shops, libraries)
- Not conduct sensitive client calls or video meetings in locations where the conversation can be overheard
- Lock screens when moving to another room even at home

---

## 8. Compliance Verification

- Supervisors conduct monthly clean desk spot checks (randomized, unannounced)
- Results logged by department and shared with management quarterly
- Persistent non-compliance escalated to HR for formal disciplinary action
- Physical security audits conducted semi-annually as part of ISO 27001 compliance

---

## 9. Exceptions

Employees who require an exception to any element of this Policy (e.g., specific hardware requirements) must submit a written exception request to the IT Security team with manager approval. Exceptions are reviewed quarterly.

---

## 10. Consequences

Non-compliance with this Policy may result in disciplinary action as outlined in the Acceptable Use Policy. In BPO environments, clean desk violations may be reportable to clients under contractual security obligations.
